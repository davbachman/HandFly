## Brief description

HandFly is a browser flight game controlled by MediaPipe hand tracking. Hold an open hand in front of the camera, tilt it like a joystick, and fly through practice airspace, an obstacle course, or a balloon shooting gallery.

## Instructions for use

Open the app, choose Flying Practice, Shooting Gallery, or Obstacle Course, and allow camera access when prompted. Show an open hand with fingers spread and aimed at the camera, then tilt left or right to bank and tip your fingers up or down to climb or dive. Make a fist to release hand control; use WASD or arrow keys to steer from the keyboard, Space to fire in shooting modes, Esc to return to the menu, H for the camera debug view, F for fullscreen, and M to toggle sound.
